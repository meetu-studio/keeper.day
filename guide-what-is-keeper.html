Meet Keeper: A Photo That Takes Photos

A pocket digital camera with a 3.7-inch six-color e-paper screen—built so your favorite photographs stay out in the open, not buried behind notifications.

A photo that takes photosHow Keeper Takes a PhotoWhy Six-Color E-Paper Feels Like a Real PrintCarry It, Stand It, or Stick It on the FridgeOnline on Its Own—No Phone RequiredEight Looks, One Camera—And a Thoughtful GiftKeeper Specifications & Buying DetailsFrequently Asked Questions
Keeper by Meetu Studio is a photo that takes photos—technically, a pocket digital camera with a 3.7-inch six-color e-paper screen. It does not print paper film and does not require a phone or companion app. Frame on the small viewfinder, press the physical red shutter, and your picture stays on a matte, print-like color display you can hang on a bag, set on a desk, or stick to your fridge.

Most of the photos we take today disappear into a phone camera roll within minutes.

Yet the moments we actually care about are small and close: a friend turning around mid-laugh on a walk, brunch on a sunlit table, your dog pausing in the autumn leaves, or a family leaning in around birthday candles. Those photographs deserve to stay out in the open, not buried behind notifications.

Keeper is built around one simple idea: a photo that takes photos. You carry it with you, raise it when something is worth keeping, press the red shutter, and let the picture stay right there on the front of the camera—matte, calm, and visible all day.

01

How Keeper Takes a Photo

Keeper works on its own, with no phone or app in between:

  1. Frame it in the small screen. Point Keeper toward the person or place in front of you. A dedicated small IPS LCD viewfinder sits in the lower-right corner, separate from the main photo display.
  2. Press the red shutter. One tactile round red button in the lower-left corner takes the photograph and saves it locally to a microSD card.
  3. Keep the photo in view. The picture appears on the 3.7-inch color e-paper display and stays there—on the café table, on your tote strap, or on your desk when you get home.

No feeds, no pop-ups, no menus getting in the way of a smile.

02

Why Six-Color E-Paper Feels Like a Real Print

Phone screens glow at you until they go dark. Keeper uses a 3.7-inch, 480 × 720 six-color e-paper screen instead:

  • Matte and glare-free. It reflects the natural light of the room or outdoors, reading like a printed photograph rather than a backlit phone screen.
  • Holds the image with power off. E-paper only draws power when the picture changes, so your photo stays visible even when the camera is off.
  • Charge about twice a year. With everyday photography, Keeper needs charging roughly twice a year over USB-C (actual battery life varies with how often you shoot, refresh the screen, and go online).

03

Carry It, Stand It, or Stick It on the Fridge

A camera you keep close should stay part of your day after the shutter clicks:

  • Loop it on your bag or wrist. Every Keeper ships with a lanyard in the box so it rides along on a tote handle, backpack strap, or wrist—always ready for the next photo walk.
  • Stick it on the fridge. Keeper is magnetic, snapping cleanly onto a refrigerator or metal surface like a living photo magnet.
  • Lean it on your desk. Rest it beside your coffee or notebook so the people and days you photographed stay in sight.

04

Online on Its Own—No Phone Required

When you want to move or share photos, you still do not need to plug Keeper into a phone:

  • Export without a phone. Keeper connects to the internet by itself and exports your photos on its own.
  • Receive photos from family & cloud sync. Beyond what you shoot yourself, Keeper can receive photos sent remotely by family and supports cloud sync—bringing a loved one’s snapshot straight onto your screen.

Photography always comes first; staying connected simply keeps your photos moving effortlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Keeper print physical photos?

No. Keeper does not use instant film or print paper photos. Every photo appears on the built-in 3.7-inch six-color e-paper screen, which looks matte like a real print and stays visible without power.

Do I need a smartphone or app to take or export photos?

No. Keeper shoots completely on its own using its small viewfinder and red shutter, saves to a microSD card, and connects online by itself to export photos without needing a phone.

Are all eight Keeper looks the same camera inside?

Yes. All eight exteriors have the exact same camera, color e-paper display, and red shutter at US$199 each (plus US$30 worldwide shipping, US$229 USD total).

How often do I need to charge Keeper?

Because e-paper only uses power when changing images, Keeper needs charging roughly twice a year via USB-C for everyday photography. Actual battery life depends on how often you shoot, refresh the display, and go online.

Where can I buy Keeper as a gift?

You can pick any of the eight looks directly at keeper.day/camera.html or keeper.day/styles.html and check out in USD via Stripe. Each camera ships in a gift box with a lanyard and manual. Questions about international delivery or gifting? Email support@keeper.day.
