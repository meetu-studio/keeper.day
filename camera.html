The red button takes the photograph

Pick it up. Take a photo.

  1. 01 · FRAME IT

    Who are you photographing?

    Raise your camera toward the person or scene. Use the small LCD viewfinder to compose your picture.

  2. 02 · TAKE IT

    Make it a photograph.

    Use the red physical shutter to take the shot. Keeper takes photographs on its own and stores them on microSD.

  3. 03 · KEEP IT

    Your shot, still in sight.

    The larger color e-paper screen gives a photograph you took a paper-like place on the camera.

Screen photos are illustrative. For card requirements, transfer, charging or detailed camera controls, contact us before purchase.

Questions about Keeper.

All questions & support ↗
What will I pay?+

The camera and package are US$199 USD, plus US$30 worldwide shipping: US$229 USD total. Stripe may offer local-currency pricing with a different converted amount; choose USD to pay the listed USD amount. Contact us about any destination-specific duties before ordering.

When will my order arrive?+

For United States orders, allow 3 business days for handling and 15 business days in transit, Monday to Friday. For other destinations or a specific delivery date, email support@keeper.day before ordering.

Does it print photos?+

No. Your photograph appears on a color e-paper display. Keeper does not make physical prints.

Do I need a phone or app?+

You can take photographs with Keeper without a phone. Photos are stored on microSD. Do not assume wireless connectivity or app features are included; ask us about your intended transfer workflow before buying.

What if I change my mind after delivery?+

You can return an unused item in its original packaging within 14 days. For change-of-mind returns, the camera price (US$199 USD) is refundable, but the original US$30 shipping charge is not; you pay return shipping. The studio pays return shipping for quality issues. See the purchase terms for the full policy.

How do I choose an exterior?+

Choose a look on the product page. ST designs are individual looks. For P designs, select A (left) or B (right); each order contains one camera with the chosen exterior, not two. All choices have the same US$199 camera price plus US$30 shipping.
