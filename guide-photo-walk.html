FIELD NOTES

Take your camera for a walk

A small route, a familiar face and something you almost walked past.

Choose an easy route.Make a three-photo story.Let people be themselves.Carry it with care.Choose a photograph to keep nearby.

01

Choose an easy route.

A familiar street, campus path or neighborhood park is enough. Leave time to stop. Notice a patch of light, a doorway or a friend turning around instead of trying to cover every place.

02

Make a three-photo story.

Photograph the place, the person and one small detail. A wide view gives context; a closer view shows what caught your attention. You do not need a special occasion to make a small set of photographs.

03

Let people be themselves.

Ask before photographing someone. Make room for a conversation or a laugh, and keep the camera ready without asking everyone to pose. For pets, let the animal come to a comfortable position.

04

Carry it with care.

Use the supplied lanyard according to the included manual. Keep the camera away from water and rough handling; this guide does not claim a waterproof rating or a tested drop resistance.

05

Choose a photograph to keep nearby.

Back at home, choose the picture that brings you back to that walk. The color e-paper display gives it a place on the camera while the rest of the photographs remain on your storage card.
