FIELD NOTES

Get to know color e-paper

Why the photograph looks different from the small viewfinder.

Two screens, two jobs.A photograph in the light around you.Still means still.Keeping a picture is not taking a picture.A camera that comes home with you.

01

Two screens, two jobs.

The small IPS LCD helps you compose the shot. The 3.7-inch color e-paper display gives a photograph a larger, paper-like place on the camera. They use different display technologies.

02

A photograph in the light around you.

Color e-paper has a reflective, matte appearance. Its appearance depends on the surrounding light. The photographs on this website illustrate the idea and are not calibrated camera samples.

03

Still means still.

The displayed photograph is a static image. It does not play video or glow like a backlit screen. Keeping an unchanged e-paper image does not require the same continuous refreshing as an LCD.

04

Keeping a picture is not taking a picture.

Taking photographs and changing the displayed image require power. Image retention does not mean the whole camera can run forever without charging. We do not publish an untested battery-life figure.

05

A camera that comes home with you.

After photographing friends, take that same camera home and keep a favorite image nearby. Desk placement is part of living with the camera, not a promise of connected apps or automatic reminders.
