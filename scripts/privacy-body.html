Privacy notice

Last updated November 9, 2026

This notice explains how keeper.day and Meetu Studio handle information when you browse our site, contact us, or place an order.

1. Information we receive

When you order, Stripe collects your email, name, billing and shipping addresses, delivery phone number, and payment information. We can access the order information needed to manage your purchase, including payment status, amount, currency, receipt reference, shipping details, and limited payment-method details such as card brand and last four digits. We do not receive your card security code or full card number through this website.

When you email us or use chat, we receive the contact information, messages, and attachments you choose to provide. Please share only information needed to help with your request.

Our website and service providers may receive technical and usage information such as IP address, browser and device type, approximate location, referring page, pages viewed, and interactions with the site.

2. How we use information

We use order and contact information to process payments, manage orders, arrange delivery, send receipts and order updates, answer support requests, handle cancellations and returns, prevent fraud, and maintain required business records. Usage information helps us understand visits and improve the site.

We may record that a purchase button was clicked. A click or return visit is not treated as a confirmed sale; Stripe's payment status is the source for order payment records.

3. Services used on this website

  • Stripe hosts checkout, processes payments and refunds, stores order information, and delivers payment communications. Its own privacy practices also apply. Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • HubSpot provides website chat and contact tools. It may use cookies or identifiers to maintain conversations and associate messages with your contact details. HubSpot Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics measures visits and interactions. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Pixel measures page visits and supports advertising measurement and audience tools. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity helps us understand page use through interaction analysis, heatmaps, and session-replay tools. Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the static website and may process technical request logs to operate and secure hosting. GitHub Privacy Statement.

Payment details are entered on Stripe's domain. Our website analytics scripts do not run on that Stripe-hosted checkout page. We share necessary shipping and contact information with delivery providers when fulfilling an order.

4. Cookies and your choices

Our optional Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity and HubSpot scripts stay off until you choose Allow optional. Essential only keeps shopping, email support and the secure Stripe checkout available without those optional website services. We store your choice in browser local storage for up to 180 days. Use Cookie settings in the footer at any time to change it. Withdrawing consent reloads the page without optional services and removes the site's known optional first-party cookies where the browser allows it; third-party providers may retain previously collected data under their own policies. Stripe applies its own checkout privacy and cookie controls. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings and use provider opt-out tools.

We also save a language you choose in browser local storage so future visits can use it. This interface preference does not enable optional analytics, advertising or chat. You can change the language at any time or remove the preference by clearing this site’s storage.

You can contact us by email if you prefer not to use website chat. Order, shipping, refund, and important product-update messages are service communications needed to manage your purchase.

5. Retention, access, and security

We keep order and support information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, resolve requests, and meet accounting or legal recordkeeping obligations. Service providers may retain records under their own policies. Information may be processed in countries other than where you live.

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by emailing support@keeper.day. We may need to verify your identity and may need to retain some records for existing orders or legal obligations. Please do not send sensitive payment credentials to us.

6. Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact Meetu Studio at support@keeper.day or through website chat. We will update the date on this page when this notice changes.
