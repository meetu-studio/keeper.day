01

Start with the person, not the settings.

Choose someone or something you want to remember. Look for steady, comfortable light and a background that gives your subject some space.

02

Use the small screen to frame.

Hold Keeper so that the camera faces your subject. The small IPS LCD is the viewfinder; the larger color e-paper screen is for the photograph. Keep your fingers away from the screens and hold the camera steadily.

03

Take the photograph with the red shutter.

Use the red physical shutter to take your picture. Keeper can take photographs without a phone. Refer to the included manual for the controls on your particular camera; this guide does not assume a press-and-release shortcut.

04

Let a photograph stay with you.

Photos are stored on microSD. A photograph on the color e-paper display can remain in sight while you carry the camera or put it on your desk. The image is displayed on the camera, not printed onto paper.

05

Before a trip or a gift.

Check your card, power and operation before heading out. Card inclusion, supported storage, transfer and charging details should be confirmed with support rather than inferred from a website photograph.
