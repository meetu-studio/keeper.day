1. What you are buying

Keeper is a standalone color e-paper camera sold by Meetu Studio. The standard package contains one camera, one gift box, one lanyard and one instruction manual. This is a one-time purchase of an in-stock product, not a subscription.

Your selected ST or P exterior is included with the camera. For a P design, choose A (left) or B (right); each purchase contains one camera, not both. Two-camera bundles, companion apps and wireless photo-sharing features are not included. Interface pages show development concepts.

The camera costs US$199. Flat worldwide shipping costs US$30 per standard one-camera order. The USD checkout amount is US$229. Review the currency, items and final amount on Stripe before paying. Destination customs may assess separate duties or taxes; these are not part of the quoted US$229. Contact us before ordering if you need clarification for your destination.

Stripe processes payment on its hosted checkout page. It may offer an optional local-currency price that differs because of currency conversion. Select USD for the stated US$229 amount; your card issuer may apply its own currency-conversion or cross-border fees. We do not collect your full card number or security code on keeper.day.

Stripe's successful-payment confirmation and receipt document your purchase. Opening checkout, clicking Buy Keeper or returning to the website does not prove that payment succeeded. Your card statement uses the descriptor KEEPER.DAY.

3. Worldwide shipping

We offer worldwide shipping to the destinations selectable at checkout. Enter a complete shipping address, name, email and delivery phone number. If your destination is not listed, contact support before ordering. Contact us promptly to correct an address before shipment.

The camera is in stock. United States orders have an estimated handling time of 3 business days and an estimated transit time of 15 business days, for an estimated total of 18 business days. Handling and transit days are Monday to Friday. For other destinations or a specific delivery date, email us before placing your order. Delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees. We will send shipping information when the order is dispatched.

4. Cancel before shipment

You may cancel at any time before your order ships. We will refund the full camera price and US$30 shipping charge to the original payment method. Email support@keeper.day with your order email and Stripe receipt reference.

5. Returns & quality issues

Within 14 days after delivery, you may request a return or exchange if the camera is unused and in its original packaging. We accept exchanges and do not charge a restocking fee. Contact us first for instructions and the return address. Returns are sent by mail; arrange the return shipment as instructed by our support team. Do not send a return to an address in payment records without checking with us.

For a return unrelated to product quality, the buyer pays return shipping. For a quality issue, we cover return shipping. If the camera arrives damaged or has a quality problem, send a description and, where useful, photos so we can arrange a resolution.

For an eligible change-of-mind return, we refund the US$199 camera price after inspecting the returned item; the original US$30 outbound shipping charge is not refundable, and you pay return shipping. For quality issues, contact us so we can arrange the appropriate resolution and cover return shipping. Refunds are initiated to the original payment method within 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Your bank or payment provider determines when the refund appears.
