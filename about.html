MEETU STUDIO · KEEPER.DAY

For the photograph you want to keep close.

Keeper is a pocket camera with a color e-paper display. Its story starts with taking a photograph.

Start with a moment.

A friend turning around, a familiar face across the table, a pause on a walk. We build the story of Keeper around the person taking that photograph.

Let the photograph stay.

The red shutter is for taking the picture. The paper-like display gives a photograph a place on the camera afterward. Carry it out, bring it home, keep it nearby.

Make the camera your own.

Different exteriors leave room for different tastes. The original camera structure stays the same across individual and paired designs.
